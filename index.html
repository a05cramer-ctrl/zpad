Why Zcash, why now

Coins quoted in ZEC already lead the asset-paired boards on Solana. ZPAD makes that the default: every coin on it is paired with ZEC, and the trading fees are paid back to holders in ZEC instead of a token you have to sell.

Zcash's NU7 network upgrade activates on November 5, 2026, with faster blocks and roughly three times the payment speed. ZPAD opens the day before. Read about NU7

How a coin runs on ZPAD

  1. Launch blind

    Pay the launch fee, pick a ticker. The pool is created a few seconds later with a mint nobody has seen, so nobody can buy block zero.

  2. Trade in ZEC

    Your coin is quoted in ZEC from the first trade. Buyers come in with ZEC, which is what they want to be paid in.

  3. Holders get paid

    Half of every fee goes back to the coin's holders in ZEC, every few minutes, in proportion to what they hold. Sell before a payout and that payout goes to the holders who stayed.

  4. Fees burn $ZPAD

    A third of every fee buys $ZPAD on the open market and burns it. Small buys, on a schedule, never more than a 1% price move.

Where every fee goes

1% on every trade. The split, in order, from the first coin onward.

    $ZPAD's own trading fees are 100% buy-and-burn. Nothing from $ZPAD goes to the team wallet.

    Rules, in plain words

    Supply
    1,000,000,000 $ZPAD. Mint and freeze authority disabled.
    Team
    0% allocation. No initial buy. One deployer wallet, listed below.
    Liquidity
    Launched on pump.fun. Graduates to PumpSwap automatically.
    Tickers
    1–10 letters or numbers. First launch of a ticker is free; reuse costs 1 SOL. Ticker, name and launch post lock for 3 days.
    Payouts
    Paid in ZEC every few minutes. Amounts under $0.50 roll over to the next payout.
    Audit
    Not audited. Upgrade authority is held by the deployer and moves to a multisig after launch.

    Verify every wallet

    Everything that moves ZEC or $ZPAD runs from these addresses. Check them on Solscan before you trust a counter.
